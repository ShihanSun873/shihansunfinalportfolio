Project 01
Are You Sure
Are You Sure is a decision system wrapped in the logic of tarot cards. It does not give advice, it only gives responses. The way you click determines what you receive: a single click feels like a calm question, repeated rapid clicks become anxiety, and doing nothing becomes hesitation. The system is not reading the question itself, but the emotional state behind the moment you ask it.
Project 02
Dream, Fate, Spirit
Three words, three visual languages. DREAM — purple particles float like breathing, scattering softly when the mouse moves close, drifting back like memory. FATE — gold particles locked in geometric precision, pulled by invisible nodes, forming threads that feel impossible to escape. SPIRIT — everything burns upward with additive blending; move the cursor higher and the word ignites, sending embers into the void.
Project 03
Album Your Way
The color palette comes from the five members of Roselia — Yukina's deep violet, Sayo's turquoise, Lisa's dark red, Rinko's white, and Ako's rose pink. Each color has a different emotional feeling attached to it, but together they create something dramatic without becoming overwhelming. Users can choose a single member's color to let one tone shape the entire composition, or use all five together, where the colors spread across the edges based on direction — almost like five different voices forming one image.
Project 04
FPS Warmup Quiz
An interactive quiz website built to help FPS players warm up before matches through targeted questions about game sense, mechanics, and strategy. The system adapts based on responses, turning preparation into something closer to a ritual than a checklist.
